How this roster works

The system keeps employees, pods, brands, processes and assignments in SQLite. When a month is generated, every required process/day is checked against assigned employees who are not on leave and are not on their weekly off.

Important: a coverage gap is never silently filled. It is shown as a conflict so the operations manager can add a backup resource or change the rule.
